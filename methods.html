How every number was made

Methods & data

Everything on this site comes from NOAA's public hurricane data files and short shell scripts in the repository, which anyone can rerun.

Data

  • Model aids (a-deck). ftp.nhc.noaa.gov/atcf/aid_public/aal092026.dat.gz, the file NHC publishes with every model forecast for this storm. The analysis uses the 12Z 8 Oct 2026 cycle. The 06Z cycle supplies the raw GEFS members and Google DeepMind's raw run (GDMN).
  • Best track (b-deck). ftp.nhc.noaa.gov/atcf/btk/bal092026.dat. For a live storm this is the operational best track, which is preliminary and is revised after the season.
  • Past seasons. ftp.nhc.noaa.gov/atcf/archive/: all Atlantic a-decks and final best tracks for 2023, 2024 and 2025.
  • Map. Natural Earth coastlines and U.S. Census state boundaries, via the world-atlas and us-atlas packages.

The files use the comma-separated ATCF "a-deck" (forecasts) and "b-deck" (best track) format. Field positions were read from the files themselves: cycle, model ID, forecast hour, latitude, longitude, maximum wind, pressure, storm type. Wind speeds are 1-minute sustained winds in knots. Rows repeat once per wind-radii threshold, so we keep the first row for each aid and forecast hour. A value of 0 kt means either no value or a dissipated system, and we drop it.

Which aids are shown

Fig. 1 shows the early (interpolated) aids NHC forecasters have at forecast time: OFCL HCCA IVCN HFAI HFBI HWFI HMNI CTCI AVNI AEMI UKX2 CEM2 SHIP DSHP LGEM GDMI NNIC OCD5. The 12-h-old duplicates (HFA2, HWF2, GDM2) are left out to reduce clutter. Model families follow how each model works, as described on the model key.

The official forecast shown is OFCL for the 12Z cycle, issued with the 15Z advisory. Many published intensity plots show OFCI instead, the previous advisory's forecast interpolated forward. For Isaias at 12Z, OFCI peaks at 82 kt and OFCL at 95 kt.

Historical NHC intensity error

To put an empirical uncertainty band on the official forecast (Fig. 1), we verified every NHC official forecast from 2023–2025 against the final best track. A forecast counts only when the system was a tropical or subtropical cyclone at both the start and the verifying time, roughly as NHC does in its own verification. Error is official minus observed. The quantiles are coarse because NHC forecasts and best-track intensities are rounded to 5 kt. These are all-storm errors. Errors for storms approaching land, or undergoing rapid intensification, can be larger. Treat the band in Fig. 1 as "typical official error," not a probability forecast for Isaias. As a check, the mean absolute errors are close to the magnitudes NHC reports in its official verification.

Spread climatology

Model spread is the sample standard deviation of the forecast intensity across a fixed set of eight aids, all present every season from 2023 to 2025: HFAI HFBI HWFI HMNI CTCI AVNI DSHP LGEM. A cycle and lead time count when at least six of the eight have a forecast. Fig. 5 compares Isaias's 12Z spread with every cycle that began at hurricane strength (official intensity at least 64 kt at the start): 371, 365 and 344 cycles at +24, +48 and +72 h.

Choices that would change the answer: a different aid set, including weaker storms (across all cycles, Isaias ranks in the 77th, 43rd and 32nd percentiles), or conditioning on cycles near landfall. We did not condition on landfall. Spread in knots naturally shrinks as storms weaken over land.

Early verification for Isaias

The "What has already verified" table scores every forecast from earlier cycles whose valid time has passed, using the operational best track through 12Z 8 Oct (tools/early_verify.awk, output in data/early_verify.csv). Averages cover a homogeneous sample: lead times of +12 to +48 h, restricted to the cycle and lead time pairs where all 14 compared aids have a forecast (12 pairs). Track error is great-circle distance in nautical miles. The 48-hour track positions quoted from the 12Z Tuesday cycle use raw (non-interpolated) output: GDMN, HFSA, HFSB, AVNO, AEMN, TVCN. The sample is small and the best track is preliminary.

Reproduce it

Requires bash, curl and GNU awk, for example Git Bash on Windows.

tools/build_data.sh al092026 ISAIAS 2026100812
tools/build_data.sh al092026 ISAIAS 2026100806
tools/spread_climatology.sh 2023 2024 2025
tools/official_errors.sh 2023 2024 2025
tools/summarize.sh al092026 2026100812
awk -F', *' -v bfile=data/raw/bal092026.dat -f tools/early_verify.awk data/raw/aal092026.dat > data/early_verify.csv
