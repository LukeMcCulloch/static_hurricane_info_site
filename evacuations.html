The Alabama order, in full

Supplemental proclamation by the Governor of Alabama, 8 October 2026: I. Mandatory evacuation of coastal tourists and visitors. The operative text, as signed:

Effective today, October 8, 2026, at 2:30 p.m. CDT, all non-residents, tourists, and visitors in the covered areas shall immediately evacuate themselves from the covered areas until the state of emergency expires or this order is modified in writing.
For purposes of this provision, “covered areas” means (1) all locations in Baldwin County south of the Gulf Intracoastal Waterway, including the City of Gulf Shores and the City of Orange Beach, and (2) all of Zone 1 in Mobile County, including all of Dauphin Island and parts of Bayou La Batre.

Signed proclamation (PDF) · Governor's press release. The press release names only Dauphin Island in Mobile County; the proclamation covers all of Zone 1.
